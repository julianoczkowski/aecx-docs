The crew in 3D

The crew works in the 3D view with you, in three ways.

Ask the crew about the selection

Section titled “Ask the crew about the selection”

  1. Select elements in the model: one, a box, or a whole class.

  2. Right-click and choose Ask the crew….

  3. In the panel beside the model, pick a crew member and ask, or press a suggestion: What are these, and is anything open on them?, Raise an RFI about these, Who should own a problem here?, Check these against the specification. The microphone lets you ask by voice.

The crew member gets your question with what you selected: each element’s class, name, GlobalId and model. It answers in the panel. Its cards appear there to confirm or dismiss, and a walk-through it lays out opens with one press. Open the whole conversation continues it on the crew member’s page.

Walk me through it

Section titled “Walk me through it”

When a crew member finds several places worth seeing (clash groups, overdue snags, the changes in a new version), it lays them out as a walk-through. In its conversation, the walk-through shows its stops and a Walk me through it button.

The walk-through plays in the 3D view, one stop at a time:

  • Each stop moves the view. It opens its issue on its viewpoint, or selects and frames its elements: a clash group’s clashing elements, the elements that changed.
  • Its note says what to look at and what to decide.
  • Its card, when the crew member prepared one for that stop, can be confirmed or dismissed right there; confirming moves on to the next stop.
  • Moving between stops: Next and Back, the arrow keys, or the dots for each stop. Esc ends the walk-through.

Ask for one in words: walk me through the worst clash groups, walk me through what changed in the structure model, walk me through the overdue snags on level 2.

The crew on the pins

Section titled “The crew on the pins”

An issue, RFI or snag a crew member is working on carries its character on its pin in the 3D view, in place of the pin’s icon. It moves with the job: working while it looks, waiting when something is ready for you. Hover the pin to read the crew member’s note.
