AI Crew

An AI crew that owns the work

Give an RFI to the RFI Chaser, a submittal to the Submittal Reviewer, a meeting’s actions to the Meeting Secretary. Each crew member keeps what it is handed until it is done: it looks straight away, looks again whenever the item changes and on the dates it sets itself, and tells you when the job is done.

Ask one a question in a comment and it replies in the thread. Select elements in 3D and ask about them, and it walks you through what it found, stop by stop. It works as you, with only its own tools, and everything it prepares waits for your OK.

Meet the AI Crew →

  • Owns a job until it is done

    Hand to crew on an issue, RFI, snag, submittal or meeting. It chases, checks and prepares, and closes the job when the goal is met.

  • Answers where the work is

    @RFI Chaser is there anything in the drawings on this? It replies in the thread, in its own name.

  • Works with you in 3D

    Right-click a selection and ask the crew. Walk through its findings stop by stop, deciding each card where it is.

  • Learns how your team works

    Dismiss a card and say why: it remembers. Its notes are yours to read and edit.

Thirteen crew members ready to start, from RFIs and clashes to submittals, meetings, handover and the work along a road or railway. See the templates →

Assistant

An assistant that answers in 3D

The app opens on the assistant. Ask show the doors on level 2, what changed in the structure model or how many walls have no fire rating, and the answer comes back as the elements in a 3D view, an exact count, or a list of issues to open.

Ask compare my projects and every project appears side by side, with its models, issues, RFIs, clashes and model health. Counts and geometry are worked out by AECX itself, not guessed, and every change the assistant suggests waits on a card for you to confirm.

Chatting with the assistant →

  • Across every project

    My open issues in every project, RFIs waiting on me, which projects have open issues: one answer, grouped by project, with each item one click from its 3D view.

  • Inside one project

    Each project has its own assistant at the top of its page rail. It works in that project only and keeps that project's conversations together.

  • Work it does for you

    Automations run an instruction on every new model version or on a schedule: what changed, overdue RFIs, the Friday coordination summary.

  • Open to other agents

    Claude and other MCP clients can connect with your own sign-in and use the same tools and 3D widgets, under your role.

Models

The whole federation, in the browser

Upload IFC 2x3, 4 and 4.3 models and LAS, LAZ or E57 laser scans, and open them together as one building in any current browser. The viewer draws with WebGPU (WebGL2 as a fallback): up to four panes, section boxes and cut planes, filters on any property, a full measuring suite, first-person walk with collision, sun studies and escape-route distances.

Put the project on the map with a real coordinate system, and its site context and interest zones stand around the models.

Opening the federation →

  • Find clashes

    Rule-based hard, clearance and duplicate tests, grouped by root cause, with a picture per group and statuses that carry over between runs.

  • Check models

    buildingSMART IDS 1.0, ten official validation rules, your own practice rules and a 0–100 health score on every upload.

  • See what changed

    Consecutive versions are compared automatically: added green, modified amber, deleted red, with quantity changes for the commercial team.

  • Run issues

    Issues with viewpoints and markup, a table and a board, live sync, offline work, and BCF 2.1 / 3.0 in and out.

Together

The whole team, live

Everyone who opens a project sees it in the same state, and sees changes as they are made: a new issue, a comment, a status, a drawing set, a file.

The header shows who is here and on which page, the 3D view shows where each of them is looking from, and Follow moves your camera with theirs, so a review goes through the model together with no screen sharing.

Working together →

  • Who is here

    Avatars in the header for everyone in the project, with the page they are on, and their cameras as pins in 3D.

  • Follow through the model

    Follow a colleague's camera from your own browser: a coordination meeting where everyone looks at the same thing.

  • Changes as they are made

    Issues, comments and statuses merge live, field work merges when back online, and new models, drawings, photos and files appear in everyone's lists.

  • The right people, asked

    Mentions, assignments and RFIs land in each person's Needs attention, with what they need to decide.

Infrastructure

Roads, railways and bridges, along the line

Infrastructure is found by chainage, not by storey. Bring in alignments from IFC 4.3 or LandXML and AECX locates everything along the line: the 3D view shows the chainage under the pointer, issues and RFIs sort from one end to the other, and the assistant answers open issues between km 0 and km 1.

Infrastructure projects →

  • Sections along the line

    A long section against the ground and the models, and a cross section that steps along the scheme 10 or 50 m at a time.

  • Clearance and earthworks

    Sweep a headroom or rail gauge along the line and list what intrudes. Work out cut, fill and the mass haul against the ground.

  • As-built vs design

    A surveyor's surface against the design, coloured on the model, with the share in tolerance and the worst places by chainage.

  • Possessions and the linear plan

    Utilities from GeoJSON, possessions and lane closures as dated zones, and a time-chainage chart of the works.

Construction

Construction workflows on the model

RFIs, submittals, drawings, photos, documents, meetings, field work and the site diary sit on the same elements as the models, so a question, a snag or a drawing pin always opens in 3D where it belongs.

RFIs →

  • RFIs and submittals

    Formal questions with an official answer and whose turn it is; submittals tied to the elements they cover, reviewed against the model.

  • Drawings

    PDF sets split into sheets, title blocks read, markups and pinned issues, and plans placed on their storey in 3D.

  • Photos and 360° walks

    One photo library per project: pinned on drawings, 360° walks on the plan, two dates side by side, and the model turning with a 360° photo.

  • Files and naming standards

    Documents in folders with versions, previews and markup. ISO 19650 names checked per folder, a holding area for names that do not comply, suggested names, and files attached to any issue, drawing, model or photo.

  • Meetings

    Agendas built from the project's open items, decisions and actions per item, and minutes issued with a transmittal.

  • Field and diary

    Snags, checklists and progress on a tablet, offline too, and one diary entry a day with the weather at the site.
