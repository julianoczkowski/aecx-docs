Finding your way around

AECX has two places: home, where you talk to the assistant and see what waits for you across all your projects, and a project, where its models, issues and construction pages live.

Home: the assistant

Section titled “Home: the assistant”

The app opens on a new conversation with the assistant. Ask about any of your projects, or pick one under the message box. The suggestions below it are a good first question: My open issues in every project, RFIs waiting on me, Which projects have open issues? or Compare my projects.

The sidebar on the left holds:

Item What it is
New chat A fresh conversation. The AECX wordmark at the top does the same
Portfolio crew Crew members who work across your organisation’s projects, when it has one that works for you. See Portfolio crew
Needs attention What waits for you across projects, with a count. See Needs attention
Projects Every project you belong to, and New project
Automations What the assistant runs for you on new versions or a schedule. See Automations
Recents Your earlier conversations, newest first, with a search box once there are many. Only you see them
Your name The account menu: organisation settings, email preferences and Sign out. See Signing in

The panel button at the top of the sidebar hides it; the same button in the corner brings it back. On a phone the sidebar opens over the page.

Note

If the assistant is not set up on your server, home is the projects page instead, and the sidebar has no New chat or Recents.

Inside a project

Section titled “Inside a project”

Open a project from Projects. It opens on the 3D viewer with every model loaded as one building.

  • The header shows the project’s name, number and your role. On the right: who else is in the project now (click an avatar to follow their camera), the sparkle for the project’s assistant, the compass for the guided tour, notifications and your account. The AECX icon on the left goes back to your projects.
  • The page rail runs down the left. Assistant sits at the top: the same assistant, working in this project only. Below it come the project’s pages and, after a divider, the construction pages. Settings is at the foot.
Rail page What it is for
Assistant Ask about this project only, with its earlier conversations listed. See Chatting with the assistant
Viewer The federated 3D view
Models Upload, browse and manage models and their versions
Issues The issue table and board
Clash Clash tests and their results
Checks IDS, validation rules, custom rules and model health
Compare Differences between two versions of a model
Packages Information packages handed to others
RFIs Formal questions with an official answer
Drawings The sheet register, markups, pins and sheets placed in 3D
Submittals Product data, shop drawings and samples for review
Meetings Agendas from the project, minutes and actions
Field Snags, checklists and progress on site
Diary One site diary entry per day
Changes Change events with quantity deltas
Settings The project’s details, site, zones, coordinate system, members, channels and export. See Project settings

Put the pages in your order

Section titled “Put the pages in your order”

The pages can go in any order, across the divider too.

  • Drag a page to a new place. A line shows where it will land.
  • Or focus a page and press Alt+↑ or Alt+↓ to move it one place.
  • Right-click the rail and choose Reset order to go back to the default.

The order is kept in your browser, for every project. The Assistant and Settings stay where they are.

The 3D viewer

Section titled “The 3D viewer”

The viewer pages (Viewer, Clash, Checks, Compare, Packages and Field) share one 3D workspace, so the models stay loaded as you move between them.

  • The toolbar runs along the top of the view: select and box select, section box and cut at face, x-ray, colour by, hide, isolate and show all, fit, views, display, panes, walk, plan, studio, escape routes, site context, interest zones, measure, the elements table and the shortcut list. New issue from this view is at its end.
  • The left panel has three tabs: Models (the model tree), Filters and Drawings (the sheets placed in 3D).
  • The right panel shows the selected element’s properties, or the page’s own panel (clash groups, check results, changes).

See Navigation and panes and Selection, tree and properties.

Guided tours

Section titled “Guided tours”

AECX explains itself. Two guided tours ring each part of the screen in turn and say what it is for:

  • The app tour starts the first time you open AECX. It covers the assistant and its suggestions, the project picker, your portfolio crew (or, if your organisation has none, what the AI Crew is), Needs attention, your projects, Automations (its templates and your automations), your conversations and the account menu. At the end, Open a project takes you into your first project.
  • The project tour starts the first time you open a project’s viewer. It covers the project’s assistant, the AI Crew (its page, what it is working on and how to add a crew member), the rail and how to reorder it, the 3D toolbar, moving around and right-clicking, the Models, Filters and Drawings tabs, the model tree, properties, every page of the rail and the notifications bell.

  1. Press Next (or → or Enter) to go on, and Back (or ←) to go back.

  2. Press the cross (or Skip on the first card), or Esc, to end the tour.

  3. Play it again at any time from the compass: at the top of the sidebar for the app tour, or in a project’s header for that project’s tour.

A tour plays by itself only once per browser.
