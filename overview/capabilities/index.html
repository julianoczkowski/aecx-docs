Capabilities at a glance

A short list of what AECX does today. Each line links to the guide that explains it.

Assistant and agents

Section titled “Assistant and agents”
  • The assistant is home: the app opens on a conversation. Ask in words and see answers in 3D. Chatting with the assistant
  • Answers across projects: every project side by side, and one list of issues or RFIs grouped by project. Answers across projects
  • A project’s own assistant at the top of its rail, with its conversations kept together. In a project
  • Changes are proposed as cards that you confirm. Confirmable changes
  • Set up a project by conversation, or let the assistant fill in the New project page. Setting up a project
  • Bots, the project’s AI teammates: each with a job, its own tools, routines it runs on its own and memory you can edit, and a character that shows what it is doing. Eight templates, or start from scratch. Bots
  • Automations on every new model version or on a schedule, managed on their own page with ready-made templates. Automations
  • AI off, or kept in your region, per organisation, with a record of every call. Privacy controls
  • Connect Claude and other agents through the MCP server. Connect agents

Getting around

Section titled “Getting around”
  • Guided tours of the app and of a project, played again from the compass. Finding your way around
  • A project page rail you can put in your own order. Put the pages in your order
  • Needs attention: everything waiting for you, across projects. Needs attention
  • Notifications in the app, by email, as a daily digest, in Slack or Teams, and as signed JSON webhooks. Notifications

Projects, models and federation

Section titled “Projects, models and federation”
  • New project on a full-screen page: address search, the site on a map, interest zones, the coordinate system and invitations. Creating a project
  • Upload IFC 2x3, IFC 4 and IFC 4.3 files, LandXML alignments and ground models, and LAS, LAZ or E57 laser scans. Uploads resume after interruptions. Uploading models
  • Versions for every model (an ISO 19650 container), with a preview image, a processing log, downloads, compare and reprocess. Models
  • ISO 19650 naming checked on upload; discipline and originator filled in from the name.
  • Bulk actions on models, and remove and restore for 30 days.
  • Open the federation, or only the models you choose. Opening the federation

3D viewer

Section titled “3D viewer”
  • Orbit, pan and zoom around the point under the cursor; standard views; perspective or orthographic. Navigation and panes
  • Up to four panes, each with its own camera.
  • Selection by click, box select and select all; model tree with eye toggles, search and properties. Selection, tree and properties
  • Filters on any property and an elements table with CSV export. Filters and elements table
  • Section box and up to six cut planes, x-ray, colour by and diagnostics. Sections, x-ray and colours
  • Saved views, a storey plan minimap, and first-person walk with collision. Saved views, plan and walk
  • Studio: sun and shadows, exploded storeys, build-up and fly-throughs; escape routes. Studio and escape routes
  • A full measuring suite with snapping. Measuring

Place and context

Section titled “Place and context”
  • A coordinate reference system and base point per project, set on a site map; every model checked against them. Georeferencing
  • Interest zones (site boundary, laydown, exclusion, access) on the map and in 3D. Interest zones
  • Site context: surrounding buildings, terrain and an aerial photo or street map; move a model for everyone. Site context and placement
  • Point clouds next to the models. Point clouds

Coordination

Section titled “Coordination”
  • Issues with viewpoints, markup, comments and mentions; table and board; bulk changes; live sync and offline. Issues open on their own elements. Issues
  • Working together: who is in the project and on which page, everyone’s camera in 3D, following someone through the model, and changes on everyone’s screen as they are made. Working together
  • BCF 2.1 and 3.0 import and export, and the BCF-API 3.0. BCF and BCF-API
  • Clash detection on the server or in your browser, grouped, with statuses and reports. Clash detection
  • Model checks: IDS 1.0, buildingSMART validation, custom rules and health. Model checks
  • Compare versions in a list and in 3D. Compare versions
  • Information packages with ISO 19650 status, revisions and tracked transmittals. Information packages

Infrastructure

Section titled “Infrastructure”
  • Alignments from IFC 4.3 and LandXML, drawn in 3D with chainage ticks; the chainage under the pointer; go to a chainage. Alignments and chainage
  • Issues, RFIs and photos by chainage, filtered by a stretch of the line and sorted along it; the assistant answers open issues between km 0 and km 1. Issues and RFIs by chainage
  • Long section against the ground and the models, and a cross section that steps along the line. Long and cross sections
  • Clearance check: a headroom or gauge envelope swept along the line, intrusions listed by chainage and raised as issues. Clearance check
  • Cut and fill volumes and the mass haul, with a CSV. Cut and fill
  • As-built vs design: a survey surface against the design, coloured on the model. As-built vs design
  • GIS layers from GeoJSON, and possessions and lane closures as dated zones. GIS layers, possessions and closures
  • A linear plan: the time-chainage chart of the works. Linear plan

Construction

Section titled “Construction”
  • RFIs on elements, with a responder, ball in court, an official answer and a register. RFIs
  • Meetings with an agenda built from the project, decisions, actions and issued minutes. Meetings
  • Submittals tied to the elements they cover, with revisions, stamps and reviews by the assistant. Submittals
  • Drawings: a sheet register from PDF sets, markups, pinned issues and sheets placed in 3D. Drawings
  • Photos and 360° walks: one library for progress and 360° photos, pinned on drawings, walked on a plan, compared over time and opened in 3D, with AI captions and tags to accept. Photos and 360° walks
  • Files and naming standards: documents in folders with versions, previews and markup; ISO 19650 names checked per folder, a holding area and one Non-compliant view with suggested names; files attached to issues, drawings, models and photos. Files and naming standards
  • Field mode: snags, checklists and progress on a tablet, offline too. Field mode
  • Site diary with the weather at the site. Site diary
  • Change events with quantity deltas by class and storey. Change events

Administration

Section titled “Administration”
  • Organisations, people and roles; full-screen project settings beside the site map. Organisations · Members and roles · Project settings
  • Single sign-on (OpenID Connect or SAML) and SCIM provisioning. Single sign-on and SCIM
  • Audit log, plans, and a full project export at any time. Audit log · Plans · Data residency and export
