Saved views, plan and walk

Saved views

Section titled “Saved views”

A saved view keeps the camera, visibility, section, colour mode, edges, projection and filters.

  1. Set the view up the way you want it.

  2. Open Views in the toolbar and save the view with a name.

  3. Copy a link to share it. Anyone in the project who opens the link sees the same view.

The lock keeps a view in your own list only. It is still stored in the project.

Start view

Section titled “Start view”

Coordinators can choose the view a project opens on, for everyone: in Views, the home button beside a shared view makes it the start view. Choose it again to open on the models as they come.

A link to a view, an issue or a clash still opens on what it points to.

Plan minimap

Section titled “Plan minimap”

The toolbar map button opens a storey plan with your position on it.

  1. Choose a storey. AECX cuts the model to it and stands you on it at eye height.

  2. Click or drag on the plan to walk there. Shift-drag (or right-drag) to turn.

  3. Use the chevrons, or Page Up / Page Down, to step a storey up or down.

  4. Expand turns the map into a pane beside the 3D view, as large as it. Scroll to zoom the plan; middle-drag (or Alt-drag) to pan.

  5. All storeys removes the cut.

While the plan is open, dragging in the 3D view turns you on the spot (your marker stays put) instead of orbiting. The section box controls stay hidden while the plan stands on a storey.

FOV (in the expanded plan and in the Views menu) sets the field of view, from 30° to 110°. Double-click it for 50°.

Walk

Section titled “Walk”

Choose the footprints in the toolbar to walk through the model in first person.

While you walk, the keys and a Collision switch show at the bottom centre of the view.

Do How
Move W A S D
Run Hold Shift
Look Drag
Jump Space
Collision on or off G
Down / up (collision off) Q / E
Exit Esc
  • With collision on, walls, railings and furniture stop you, you climb steps and stairs, and you fall off edges. Doors and spaces are walked through.
  • With collision off, you fly through everything.
  • Outside the model (nothing below you), you fly either way.
  • With a site context, walk mode stands on the terrain.

Tip

Combine walk with the plan minimap: pick a storey, click where you want to stand, then walk from there.
